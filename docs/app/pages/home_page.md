# Home Page (`app/src/pages/HomePage.tsx`)

## 1. Overview
The entry page of the SPA. Contains:
- Header bar with sidebar trigger and breadcrumb title.
- Fullscreen `CreateInterviewForm` 3-Step Wizard:
  - **Step 1**: Target Role & Technical Requirements (fullscreen responsive grid and expanding textarea).
  - **Step 2**: Multi-CV Candidate Pool (drag & drop zone + candidate queue cards).
  - **Step 3**: AI Screening Winner, Pool breakdown & High-resolution Document Viewer.
- Responsive edge-to-edge layout filling 100% viewport width and height.
